# Zana Agent

**Code. Remember. Keep going.**

A terminal coding agent with persistent local memory, background workers and your choice of model.

- **Zana** — the agent, in your terminal.
- **Zana Cloud** — your sessions in the browser and on your phone *(private preview)*.

curl -fsSL https://zanaagent.app/install | sh
